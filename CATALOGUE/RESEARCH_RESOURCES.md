# Research resources: datasets, papers and open-source engineering

## Primary-source technical literature
- NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS): https://ntrs.nasa.gov/ — public metadata and full-text NASA STI, including technical reports, conference papers, journal articles, patents, images and videos. Some registered collections require NASA credentials.
- NASA Technical Standards: https://standards.nasa.gov/NASA-Technical-Standards — standards by discipline, including avionics/electronics, structures/propulsion, parts/materials, test, safety and operations.

## Official aviation data
- FAA Releasable Aircraft Database: https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/releasable_aircraft_download — downloadable registration/reference data refreshed daily; personal information suppression and data-field limitations apply.
- FAA Airworthiness Directives: https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/continued_operation/ad/issue_pub — official route to AD records; use FAA DRS for current and historical guidance.
- FAA Dynamic Regulatory System: https://drs.faa.gov/ — broad searchable regulatory and safety guidance repository.
- EASA Safety Publications / ADs and Easy Access Rules: use EASA official portals; verify current applicability and revision.
- ICAO Annexes and SARPs: access via ICAO official publication channels; many are restricted or paid.

## Open-source software and repositories
- PX4: https://github.com/PX4/PX4-Autopilot — modular open-source autopilot; SITL/HITL, MAVLink and ROS 2 integration; BSD-3-Clause.
- AeroCommons: https://aerocommons.org/ — open-source GA projects including EFIS, navigation data and avionics gateways.
- JSBSim: https://github.com/JSBSim-Team/jsbsim — flight dynamics modelling.
- OpenVSP: https://github.com/OpenVSP/OpenVSP — parametric geometry and conceptual aircraft design.
- SU2: https://github.com/su2code/SU2 — CFD and multiphysics.
- OpenMDAO: https://github.com/OpenMDAO/OpenMDAO — multidisciplinary analysis and optimization.
- FlightGear: https://github.com/FlightGear/flightgear — open flight simulator.
- ArduPilot: https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot — open autopilot stack.

## Third-party aggregation (not authority)
- OpenAirDocs: https://openairdocs.org/ — useful index/aggregation of public aviation data such as ADs, accident investigations, type certificate data, STCs, service difficulty and registry records. Validate consequential facts against FAA/NTSB/EASA primary records.

## Research protocol
For every dataset or paper, record publisher, persistent identifier/DOI, publication date, revision, access rights, licence, data dictionary, provenance, checksum, and limitations. Prefer authority-hosted data. For third-party aggregations, retain the original authority URL and do not treat aggregation as legal or technical approval.
