# Ingestion and verification workflow

1. Discover candidate document at the issuing authority or rights-holder.
2. Confirm exact title, document number, edition, revision and date.
3. Confirm platform, jurisdiction and technical scope.
4. Inspect terms of use and redistribution permission.
5. Download only where permitted.
6. Verify file type, size, readability and malware scan.
7. Calculate SHA-256 and store it in the asset manifest.
8. Extract metadata; do not infer revision from filename alone.
9. Compare with publisher's current catalogue and supersession notices.
10. Tag the document and link related standards, requirements, systems and platforms.
11. Technical review by a competent reviewer; mark limitations and known gaps.
12. Publish with status and review date.

## Download policy
- Prefer official authority/issuer URLs.
- Do not mirror paid standards or controlled manufacturer manuals without permission.
- Do not include military/export-controlled or otherwise restricted technical data.
- Do not scrape or redistribute material contrary to terms.
- Where redistribution is not allowed, retain bibliographic metadata and the official acquisition/access URL only.

## Engineering use policy
No KAAVAAK record is approved design data merely because it passed ingestion checks. For aircraft design, maintenance, repair, modification, release to service or flight operation, use applicable current approved/accepted data and authorized engineering/maintenance processes.
