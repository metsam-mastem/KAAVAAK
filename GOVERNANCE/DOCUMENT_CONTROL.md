# Document control and engineering records

## Minimum metadata
- KAAVAAK document ID
- Title and document type
- Publisher / author / issuing authority
- Exact designation and revision
- Issue / publication / effective dates
- Jurisdiction and platform applicability
- System/domain tags
- Source URL and access date
- Rights and redistribution status
- Download state and checksum (SHA-256)
- Supersedes / superseded-by relationship
- Verification owner, verification date and notes

## Document types
Textbook, lecture notes, course module, handbook, regulation, certification specification, AMC/GM, advisory circular, standard, technical report, research paper, maintenance manual, illustrated parts catalogue, service bulletin, airworthiness directive, test procedure, drawing, dataset, software source and training assessment.

## Status lifecycle
CANDIDATE → SOURCE_VERIFIED → RIGHTS_REVIEWED → DOWNLOADED → INTEGRITY_CHECKED → METADATA_RECORDED → TECHNICAL_REVIEWED → PUBLISHED.

A document may be technically relevant but not legally redistributable. Keep source access, copyright status and engineering validity as separate fields.

## Change control
Never silently overwrite a controlled document. Store each revision separately, record the supersession link, preserve its checksum and update the change log. For standards, verify current revision at the issuing authority before use.

## Critical distinction
A public educational handbook is not approved maintenance data. A standard's existence does not prove applicability to a particular aircraft, modification or certification project.
