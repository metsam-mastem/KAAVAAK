## Not completed
- No bulk PDF download is claimed.
- No paid, restricted, export-controlled or manufacturer-controlled technical manuals were mirrored.
- No claim is made that every listed standard is the current revision or applicable to a specific aircraft.

## Research findings
- FAA DRS consolidates regulatory guidance and offers current and historical records.
- EASA Easy Access Rules are published in PDF, online and XML; EASA describes general reuse conditions with source acknowledgement unless otherwise stated. Its XML export carries specific limitations and is not itself the legally authentic Official Journal publication.
- NASA Technical Standards are organized by discipline, including avionics/electronics, structures/propulsion, parts/materials, test, safety and operations.
- ICAO Annex access is restricted or paid for many documents; official access routes must be used.
- AeroCommons is a useful open-source aviation lead for EFIS, navigation data and avionics integration; project-level licensing and airworthiness limitations still require review.
